Robot using free spin of bearings to calculate motion

Chat date: Oct 5 · Original chat on grok.com ↗ · Raw capture (.md)

Capture note. Included because the chat states real mechanics relations (rolling odometry, rotational dynamics with friction, a prediction residual).

Joshua's messages (verbatim)

  1. Maybe the robot can use free spin of bearings to calculate motion

Grok's response (as captured)

A free-spinning bearing is a usable motion sensor. Treat it as an unloaded integrator, not a drive element.

Idler / freewheel odometry. One bearing is not driven; it rolls with the ground or a trailing link. An encoder on that shaft counts revolutions. Distance and speed:

s=2πrN,v=2πrN˙s = 2\pi r N, \qquad v = 2\pi r \dot{N}s=2πrN,v=2πrN˙

where rrr is the rolling radius and NNN is revolutions. Like a fifth-wheel or mouse ball, the free bearing reports what the body actually did.

Coast memory. Clutch the bearing off the motor for a short window and let it freewheel. Friction bleeds its spin rate:

Iω˙=−τf(ω)I\dot{\omega} = -\tau_f(\omega)Iω˙=−τf​(ω)

If τf\tau_fτf​ is known (Coulomb plus viscous, calibrated once), the decay curve reconstructs the speed at release. Compare that free-spin reading with the driven-side encoder; the difference is slip, impact, or a bad prediction.

Predict-then-correct. Predict the free-spin rate expected if the last motion command was right; sample the bearing to get ωmeasured\omega_{\text{measured}}ωmeasured​. Residual:

e=ωpredicted−ωfreee = \omega_{\text{predicted}} - \omega_{\text{free}}e=ωpredicted​−ωfree​

Correct the hypothesis from eee, not from motor current alone (current lies when the wheel skates).

Practical limits: rrr changes with load and tire squash, so it needs slow calibration; bearing drag sets a floor (below a few rad/s the count stalls); counts per revolution must be fine enough that one tick is smaller than the correction needed; the free bearing is not a gyro (a flywheel on a gimbal is for rotation).

Minimal loop: command, predict free-spin, sample the idler encoder, correct odometry and the next command from the residual.
